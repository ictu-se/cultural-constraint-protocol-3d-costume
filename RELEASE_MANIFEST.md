# Release manifest

This archive accompanies manuscript DAACH-D-26-00311.

Included experiment groups:

- `exp02`: corrected ordinal simulator with assessable, NA, and rejected states;
- `exp03`: simulator unit tests and sensitivity analysis;
- `exp04`: mesh instrumentation diagnostic with category-balanced parent split;
- `exp05`–`exp07`: Blender/TripoSR assets, manifests, renders, and author
  technical record-generation demonstration;
- `exp08`–`exp11`: Qwen3-VL prompt, view, pairwise, and atomic-judge audits.

The archive also contains profile and evidence configurations, an explicit audit
record schema, blank human-validation instruments, source and regeneration
scripts, figure scripts, environment specifications, and checksums.

`SHA256SUMS.txt` is the machine-readable file-completeness and integrity
manifest. No completed specialist/community ratings are included. The archive
does not claim cultural construct validity or an immutable public release DOI.

Experiment `exp12` adds a multi-model atomic technical-discrimination
benchmark. Its VLM outputs are not cultural validation.
